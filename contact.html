Got questions before buying? Here are clear, straightforward answers about sizing, payments, delivery, and exchanges.

It is the fastest way to buy online in India! Simply add your chosen clogs and sizes to your bag, fill in your delivery address, and click "Place Order on WhatsApp". This instantly opens WhatsApp with a pre-filled summary message sent to our team. We confirm your order and send you tracking right there!

On the checkout page, you will see our verified UPI merchant ID (necternest@upi) and a direct UPI QR code. You can scan the QR code using Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, or CRED, or enter the UPI ID. Once paid, simply send the screenshot or transaction reference ID on WhatsApp to confirm dispatch.

NecterNest clogs follow standard Indian / UK shoe sizes (from UK 5 to UK 11). If you wear a half size (for example, UK 7.5), we recommend ordering the next size up (UK 8) for a roomy, relaxed fit. You can also click the to see exact foot lengths in centimeters.

We want you to love your clogs! If the size isn’t a perfect fit, just notify us within 7 days of delivery on WhatsApp or email. As long as the clogs are in unused condition with original packaging, we will arrange a reverse pickup and ship your replacement size with zero hassle.
