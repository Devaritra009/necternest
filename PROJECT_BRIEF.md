# PROJECT BRIEF: NecterNest Ecommerce Store

**Date:** November 21, 2026  
**Status:** Approved & Finalized  
**Brand Name:** NecterNest  
**Industry:** Footwear / Casual D2C Ecommerce (India)

---

## 1. Brand & Business Overview
- **Brand Name:** NecterNest
- **Core Product:** High-comfort, stylish clogs and foam footwear (slip-ons / slides)
- **Target Audience:** College students, young working professionals, daily commuters, and everyday comfort seekers in India looking for stylish, durable, and budget-friendly footwear.
- **Brand Personality / Vibe:** **Fresh, Playful & Ultra-Comfort** — warm, accessible, fun, and modern with an emphasis on cloud-soft cushioning and effortless daily style.
- **Domain Strategy:** Built domain-ready; prepared for immediate connection to `necternest.in`, `necternest.com`, or equivalent upon registration.

---

## 2. Catalog & Pricing
- **Catalog Size:** 10+ distinct clog styles across Men's, Women's, and Unisex options.
- **Price Range:** ₹799 – ₹999 (optimized for impulse purchases and high conversion in the Indian market).
- **Product Visuals:** High-resolution, realistic footwear mockups and lifestyle-oriented visuals for the day-1 launch, with modular image paths so real product photography can be dropped in easily later.

## 3. Pages & Store Architecture
1. **Home Page:**
   - Announcement / Offers bar at the top
   - Hero banner showcasing trending clogs and comfort features
   - "Shop by Category" (Men, Women, Unisex, New Drops)
   - Featured Clogs collection grid
   - Value props (Waterproof, Featherlight EVA, Anti-slip)
   - Customer Reviews and Social Proof
   - Newsletter / Launch discount hook
2. **Shop / Catalog Page:**
   - Full grid listing of all 10+ clog styles
   - Filter by Gender/Category (Men, Women, Unisex) and sort by Price/Popularity
   - Quick-view or direct card selection
3. **Product Detail Page (PDP):**
   - High-quality image gallery with thumbnail previews
   - Interactive Size Selector (UK / India sizes 5 to 11)
   - Quantity selector
   - "Add to Cart" and instant "Buy Now via WhatsApp" buttons
   - Product highlight tabs (Material specs, Waterproofing, Cleaning guide)
   - Interactive Size Chart Modal with foot measurement guide
4. **Cart & Checkout Drawer/Page:**
   - Real-time cart calculation with free delivery threshold indicator
   - Coupon code input (e.g., `FIRSTNEST` for launch discount)
   - Customer delivery address and contact details form
   - Instant WhatsApp order generator with pre-formatted message
5. **About Us Page:**
   - The NecterNest story: sweet comfort, ergonomic cushioning, and daily wearability
   - Quality guarantee and comfort promise
6. **Contact & FAQ Page:**
   - Interactive FAQ accordion (sizing, delivery timelines, exchange policy)
   - Customer contact form, support email, and WhatsApp helpdesk details

## 4. Visual Design & UI System
- **Theme:** **Sweet & Fresh**
  - **Primary Brand Accent:** Honey-Amber / Soft Peach (`#F59E0B` / `#F97316` / `#FEF3C7` accents)
  - **Neutral Backgrounds:** Crisp Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) with subtle off-white / light slate sections (`#F8FAFC`)
  - **Typography & Dark Elements:** Deep Charcoal / Ink Black (`#0F172A`) for high contrast and readability
- **UI Styling:** Rounded cards, soft modern drop-shadows, pill-shaped action buttons, and touch-friendly mobile layouts.
- **Responsiveness:** 100% Mobile-first responsive design tailored for mobile shoppers.

---

## 5. Ordering & Payment Architecture
- **Order Mechanism:** **Instant WhatsApp Order System + Direct UPI QR Code**
  - Allows immediate launch without merchant gateway approval delays or aggregator fees.
  - Generates an automated, structured WhatsApp message containing:
    - Order items, selected sizes, quantities, and prices
    - Customer delivery address, city, pincode, and contact number
    - Total order amount with applied discounts
  - Displays a clean UPI QR code / UPI ID on checkout for seamless mobile-to-mobile payment confirmation.
