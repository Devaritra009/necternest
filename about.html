✨ PRO TIPS

How To Care For Your NecterNest Clogs

NecterNest clogs are virtually indestructible with minimal care. Follow these quick steps to keep them looking fresh out of the box:

  1. Daily Dust & Mud: Simply wash under tap water or wipe with a damp micro-fiber cloth.
  2. Stubborn Stains: Use mild dish soap or liquid detergent with a soft brush. Do not use bleach or harsh solvents.
  3. Drying: Air-dry in shaded, ventilated room. Important: Do not leave clogs exposed to scorching midday car trunks or direct heat heaters, as high thermal exposure can cause EVA foam shrinkage.
