✨ PRO CARE TIPS

How To Keep Your Drops Looking Fresh

NecterNest clogs are ultra-durable with minimal maintenance. Follow these simple tips:

  1. Daily Dust & Mud: Rinse under running tap water or wipe with a damp microfiber cloth.
  2. Tough Stains: Use mild dish soap or liquid handwash with a soft sponge. Avoid bleach or harsh chemical solvents.
  3. Drying: Air-dry in shaded, ventilated room. Note: Never leave clogs baking in direct 45°C midday sun or locked in hot car trunks to prevent heat shrinkage.
